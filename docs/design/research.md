# UniBite Dribbble Design Research (`docs/design/research.md`)

To establish a distinctive, non-generic visual language for **UniBite** (*"Your Campus, Your Food, Your Way."*), we explored Dribbble across food ordering, canteen/campus workflows, order tracking, kitchen display systems (KDS), and empty states.

> **Copyright & Originality Note:** Patterns below were analyzed and adapted into UniBite's own campus green/orange/yellow design language (`70/20/10` ratio) with original swoosh, graduation-cap, fork, and leaf motifs. Zero external images or illustrations are copied.

---

## Curated Reference Shots & Adopted Patterns

| # | Dribbble Search / Shot Reference | Search URL / Source | Adopted Pattern (Layout, Hero, Card, Motion) | Why It Fits UniBite Users |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Order Management System** (Halil Nuroğlu) | https://dribbble.com/search/order-management-system | Multi-column status Kanban with high-contrast ticket headers, concise item×qty rows, and top search/filter bar. | Cafeteria staff scan orders from 1–2m away on counter tablets; clear column color-coding cuts cognitive load in noisy kitchens. |
| 2 | **Food Order Flow Management** (Krisna Wiyana) | https://dribbble.com/search/food-order-flow-management | Stage-based order lifecycle cards with elapsed-time indicator and single-click forward progression CTA. | Directly aligns with UniBite's `Pending → Accepted → Preparing → Ready → Completed` state machine and auto-reject countdown. |
| 3 | **FoodMagic Restaurant Order Management Dashboard** (MindInventory) | https://dribbble.com/search/restaurant-dashboard-orders | Dark collapsible navigation sidebar paired with a soft light canvas and inline QR/order lookup bar. | Anchors the 70% Campus Green palette (`#12301B` sidebar + `#F4F8F2` canvas) while letting staff look up pickups without modal hops. |
| 4 | **Food Delivery App – Order Tracking Flow** (SK Radwoan) | https://dribbble.com/search/order-tracking-ui | Horizontal segmented progress stepper with pulsing active stage and elevated QR pickup card. | Hurried students between lectures need instant plain-language status clarity ("Ready — show your QR") at a single glance. |
| 5 | **Track Order - Food & Shipment** (Atul Kaushal) | https://dribbble.com/search/food-delivery-app | Floating bottom status/cart bar + top hero greeting card with rounded 20–28px sheet geometry. | Keeps primary actions inside the one-handed thumb zone on mobile without cluttering the header. |
| 6 | **Restaurant Ordering + Kitchen Display System (KDS)** (Ronas IT) | https://dribbble.com/search/restaurant-kitchen-display | Large counter-friendly ticket buttons (`Accept` / `Reject`), clear prep-time chips, and muted vs urgent card borders. | Prevents mis-taps during peak lecture breaks and surfaces urgent pending orders with an orange/red ring. |
| 7 | **Cart Bottom Sheet & Food Menu** (Tarro / Mobile Food UI) | https://dribbble.com/search/cart-bottom-sheet-food | Collapsing cafeteria cover header with sticky category tabs, Hero image into a `28px`-radius detail bottom sheet, and sticky orange "Add – price" CTA. | Reduces ordering to ≤ 4 taps for repeat campus customers and keeps unit/total prices unmistakable. |
| 8 | **Food Empty State Illustration** (Kavita Khati) | https://dribbble.com/search/empty-state-illustration-food | Minimalist warm food-bowl / steam illustration paired with concise human copy and one recovery action. | Eliminates dead-end blank screens and pairs cleanly with UniBite's looping steam-wisp animation. |
